Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment online through our website by clicking the "Book Appointment" button, call us at +1 (608) 555-0124, or visit our clinic in person. We recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred time slot.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept all major insurance plans including Medicare, Medicaid, Blue Cross Blue Shield, United Healthcare, Aetna, Cigna, and many others. Please contact our billing department for specific questions about your insurance coverage.

Do you offer telemedicine services?

Yes, we offer telemedicine consultations for select services. You can have a virtual visit with our doctors from the comfort of your home. Please call our office to schedule a telemedicine appointment.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring your insurance card, photo ID, list of current medications, relevant medical records, and any questions you may have for the doctor. Arrive 15 minutes early to complete paperwork.

Is parking available at the clinic?

Yes, we have a dedicated parking lot for patients with free parking. There is also street parking available nearby. The clinic is wheelchair accessible.
